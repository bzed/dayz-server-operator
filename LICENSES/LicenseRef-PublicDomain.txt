Public domain dedication for the country flag SVGs
==================================================

Applies to internal/web/vendor/flags/*.svg, the 4:3 country flags of the
linuxmint ISO country flags SVG collection
(https://github.com/linuxmint/iso-country-flags-svg-collection).

The collection claims no copyright on the flags. It carries no licence file;
each SVG states the dedication in a comment at its top instead, here with the
text of de.svg (only the title and the rights holder differ between files):

    <!-- /Creative Commons Public Domain -->
    <!--
    <rdf:RDF xmlns="http://web.resource.org/cc/"
        xmlns:dc="http://purl.org/dc/elements/1.1/"
        xmlns:rdf="http://www.w3.org/1999/02/22-rdf-syntax-ns#">
    <Work rdf:about="">
       <dc:title>SVG graphic of German flags</dc:title>
       <dc:rights><Agent>
          <dc:title>Philipp Sadleder</dc:title>
       </Agent></dc:rights>
       <license rdf:resource="http://web.resource.org/cc/PublicDomain" />
    </Work>

    <License rdf:about="http://web.resource.org/cc/PublicDomain">
       <permits rdf:resource="http://web.resource.org/cc/Reproduction" />
       <permits rdf:resource="http://web.resource.org/cc/Distribution" />
       <permits rdf:resource="http://web.resource.org/cc/DerivativeWorks" />
    </License>
    </rdf:RDF>
    -->

The license element is the Creative Commons Public Domain dedication, which
permits reproduction, distribution and derivative works without conditions.
Debian's iso-flags-svg, which packages the same collection, records it as
"No copyright claimed" with "License: public-domain".

The flags are therefore in the public domain: no copyright is claimed and no
conditions, attribution or otherwise, apply to their use.
